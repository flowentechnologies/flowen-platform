Phase 2 — Product feature posts (6 posts)
============================================
Real, authenticated screenshots of the live Flowen platform — one set
from the PWS (person-who-stutters) practice experience, one from the
SLT clinician portal. Blurred UI + bold callout, same format as the
rest of the series but visually distinct from the letter cards.

Note on the demo data visible in these screenshots: the account used
("Alex Morgan") is a test account with synthetic seeded session data,
used only to populate the UI for these captures. Captions describe
the feature shown, not the specific numbers in the screenshots —
those numbers are not a real user's results and shouldn't be quoted
as one.
